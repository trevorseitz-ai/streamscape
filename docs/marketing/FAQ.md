## What is ReelDive?

ReelDive is a cinematic **streaming discovery** experience built to reduce “where is this streaming?” friction. Browse and curate titles with **availability** surfaced from mirrored catalog data—not by replacing individual streamer subscriptions.

---

## Is ReelDive a playback app? Do I watch inside ReelDive?

**No.** ReelDive is **not** an in-app **playback** hub for licensed studio catalogs across every service. Expect **discovery, availability cues, lists, and handoff cues** aligned with **`docs/depts/web-tv-parity.md`**—not a universal in-player substitute for subscriber apps.

---

## Is ReelDive a replacement for Netflix / Max / Prime Video / Disney+?

**No.** Users still maintain **subscriber relationships** with their streamers where required. ReelDive helps **orient and decide**, then typical flows hand off to the appropriate **streaming apps / sites** per catalog rules.
