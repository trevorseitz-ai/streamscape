## App Store (iOS) — canonical draft

**App name (reference):** ReelDive

**Subtitle idea (trim to ≤30 characters for App Store Connect):** Find films & where they stream

### Store description (paste-ready)

Stop bouncing between apps to answer one question: *what should I watch—and where is it actually streaming?* **ReelDive** is a modern **Stream Finder** for people who love movies: browse curated discovery rails, open rich detail with poster-perfect artwork, and see **real provider availability** at a glance—so you spend less time searching and more time watching.

**DISCOVER WHAT’S OUT THERE**  
Explore a polished Discover experience built for speed and clarity. Featured-style collections help surface titles worth your attention, while **streaming badges** show which services carry a film before you even open the sheet.

**SEE WHERE IT STREAMS**  
ReelDive mirrors an up-to-date provider catalog (with **broad mainstream and niche/premium coverage**) so availability isn’t a guess—it's reflected consistently across discovery and your profile.

**BUILT FOR HOW YOU REALLY WATCH**  
Save picks to your **Watchlist**, organize your **Library**, and use **Search** when you already know the title. Tune **Profile → My services** so what you care about stays aligned with the catalog the app maintains.

**ONE APP, YOUR SCREENS**  
The same thoughtful flow is designed to feel at home on **iPhone and Android** today—and pairs with our lean-back **Android TV** experience when you want the living-room version.

**Account that stays with you**  
Sign in to keep preferences and lists in sync via secure backend infrastructure—so returning feels familiar, not like starting over.

### Promotional text (optional, short)

Curated movie discovery with live streaming availability, watchlists, and a profile tuned to your services.
